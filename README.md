# 🐘 Smart Wildlife Detection System

#### For more [like](https://srec.ac.in/coin/blognewsdetails/national-level-hours-hackathon)

An intelligent wildlife detection system designed to help address **Human–Animal Conflict (HAC)** through early wildlife detection and monitoring.

## 🌿 About the Project

Human–animal conflicts are a growing challenge in areas where wildlife habitats overlap with human settlements, agricultural land, and transportation routes. Early detection of wildlife can provide valuable time for people to respond safely and take preventive action.

**Smart Wildlife Detection System** aims to provide a technology-driven approach to detecting wildlife and supporting early warnings before animals come into close contact with humans.

## 🎯 Problem Statement

Wild animals entering human-populated areas can result in:

* Human injuries and loss of life
* Crop and property damage
* Wildlife injuries or deaths
* Delayed response to animal movement
* Difficulty monitoring large areas continuously

Traditional monitoring methods can be difficult to operate continuously and may not provide timely detection.

## 💡 Proposed Solution

The project focuses on creating a smart monitoring system capable of detecting wildlife from a monitored environment and providing useful information about detected animals.

The overall idea is:

**Monitor → Detect → Identify → Alert → Respond**

This can help communities and wildlife authorities take preventive measures and reduce unnecessary encounters between humans and animals.

## ✨ Key Features

* 🐾 Wildlife detection
* 📷 Camera-based monitoring
* 🔍 Animal identification
* ⚡ Early detection
* 🚨 Alert-oriented monitoring
* 🌳 Support for wildlife and human safety
* 📊 Potential for continuous environmental monitoring

## 🌍 Use Cases

The system can be adapted for environments such as:

* Agricultural areas near wildlife habitats
* Forest-border villages
* Wildlife corridors
* Roads passing through wildlife regions
* Farms and plantations
* Protected forest areas
* Wildlife monitoring stations



## 🎯 Goal

The ultimate goal of the project is to use technology as an additional layer of protection for **both humans and wildlife**.

Instead of reacting after a conflict occurs, the system focuses on **detecting wildlife early and enabling preventive action**.
